🎾 HOW IT WILL WORK

💜 ABSOLUTE BEGINNERS

If you're completely new to padel or have very little match experience, you'll play on our guided beginner courts.
This will be a match-class format: you'll play real points and games while receiving guidance on rules, scoring, positioning, technique and how a padel match actually works.
No pressure and no need to know everything beforehand, this part of the event is about learning by playing and gaining confidence on court. 😊
We'll also send our Absolute Beginners the Padel Beginner Guide + recommended video privately before the event, so you can familiarise yourself with the basics.

👑 AMERICANA - BEGINNERS, IMPROVERS & INTERMEDIATE

Players with more match experience will play our Americana format:
• No fixed pairs, partners and opponents rotate throughout the event
• [MINUTES]-minute rounds
• Keep track of your own games during each round 📝
• When the round finishes, rotate courts according to the result
• Golden point at deuce (40-40), no advantage
• Win and you'll move towards Court 2 👑
• Keep winning... and you might finish the night as our Padel Queen! 👑🔥

We'll share the starting courts/groups before the event, so keep an eye on the group.

🎒 WHAT TO BRING

💧 Extra water, one bottle is included, but it's [MONTH] and you'll be playing for 90 minutes, so please bring more.
👟 Proper sports/padel shoes and comfortable sportswear.
🎾 Your padel racket and balls if you have!

No racket? Rackets can be rented at Delfos for €4. We'll contact the players who indicated that they need one and we'll also try to arrange some spare rackets where possible.

📸 We'll be taking photos and videos during the event. If you'd prefer not to appear in them, just let one of the organisers know.

⚠️ IMPORTANT

Every player matters! The courts and formats are organised around the number and experience of the participants, so please arrive on time.
If something comes up and you can't attend, Playtomic's cancellation policy applies, but please let us know as early as possible so we have time to find someone to take your place and avoid affecting the other players. 💜
