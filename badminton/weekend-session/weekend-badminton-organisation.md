# Weekend Badminton Organisation

Alongside the [Friday session](../friday-session/friday-badminton-faq.md) - run
the same way, directly by Mel, since 2026-07-29's correction - Keep Calm also
runs a **weekend badminton session**, organised the same manual way: a
WhatsApp poll, Mel booking courts based on headcount, and payment by Bizum.

---

## How It's Organised

- The organiser (**Mel**) posts a **poll in the WhatsApp group** asking who's free
  for the coming weekend.
- Based on the headcount, she books **1 or 2 courts** at the venue - typically 1
  court for around 6 people, or 2 courts once numbers reach 9-12.
- Once courts are booked, she posts the **cost per person** (calculated by
  dividing the court cost across confirmed players - typically **€5-6 per
  person**, more if multiple courts are needed) and a **Bizum** number to secure
  a spot (Revolut on request for anyone without Bizum).
- Spots are only secured once **paid**, not just by voting in the poll or RSVPing.
- Mel keeps a running **confirmed list** in the group (paid players marked with a
  checkmark) and updates it as people pay or drop out.

## Weekly Timeline

### Early in the week - Poll
- Post the weekend availability poll in the group.
- Ask people to update their availability as they see it, since courts are booked
  according to numbers.

### Mid-week - Confirm and book
- Once there's a good headcount, book 1 court (or 2, if there's enough interest)
  at the venue.
- Share the venue, cost per person, and payment details (Bizum/Revolut) in the
  group.
- Keep the confirmed list updated in the group as people pay.

### Day of - Session
- Players arrive at the venue for the booked time slot with their racket, indoor
  shoes, and water.
- Mel (or another organiser present) sorts out courts and rotations on the day.
- Note: the exact hall used at a venue can vary session to session (e.g. main
  hall vs a smaller side hall) - check the group on the day for exact directions.

## Key Details

| | |
|---|---|
| **Day / time** | Normally **Saturday morning** (roughly 9-11am or 10am-12pm depending on booking) - occasionally shifts to Sunday morning if the usual slot isn't available that week |

| **Courts** | 1-2 courts, depending on headcount |
| **Cost** | Varies by number of courts/players - typically €5-6 per person |
| **Run by** | Keep Calm directly - organised by **Mel** (same as the Friday session) |
| **Payment** | Bizum (or Revolut on request) to the organiser - spot is only secured once paid |
| **Bring** | Own racket, indoor court shoes, water |
