# Friday Badminton - FAQ

## About the Session

### What is Keep Calm Badminton?
A weekly badminton session organised through the Keep Calm community - a not-for-profit community for English speakers in Barcelona. We play every Friday evening, normally at La Mar Bella Municipal Sports Complex, organised directly by **Mel**: a WhatsApp poll, courts booked based on headcount, and payment by Bizum.

### Who is this for?
Everyone. It's a **mixed** game - ladies and gents both welcome, all ages - currently pitched at a **lower-intermediate** level. This is social badminton, not a competition, so turning up and having fun matters far more than the score.

### Is it beginner friendly?
The Friday session is aimed at a **lower-intermediate** level, so it suits people who know the basics and can hold a rally. Genuine beginners are still welcome to come along, but be honest about your level when you RSVP so the games stay enjoyable for everyone. If you're brand new, mention it in the group and we'll point you to the right session.

### Who organises the Friday session?
**Mel** organises it directly for Keep Calm - there's no external partner community involved. She posts a WhatsApp poll to gauge interest, books courts based on the headcount, and collects payment by Bizum once courts are confirmed.

## The Weekly Session

### When and where do you play?
**Friday evenings**, normally at **La Mar Bella Municipal Sports Complex**, Av. del Litoral, 86-96, Poblenou - typically in the 16:00-18:30 window, but the exact time varies week to week depending on court availability, so check the group for that week's confirmed time. See [Venue](friday-badminton-venues.md) for full details and how to get there.

### What format do you play?
**Doubles practice games** (2v2), rotating so everyone gets plenty of court time and games with different partners. The number of courts (1-3) depends on how many people sign up that week.

### How much does it cost and how do I pay?
Cost varies depending on how many courts get booked - typically **€5-6 per person**, covering the court hire and shuttlecocks. Mel shares the exact amount and a **Bizum** number (Revolut on request) once courts are confirmed for the week - your spot is only secured once you've paid.

### How many spots are there?
It depends on how many courts Mel books that week, based on interest in the WhatsApp poll - there's no fixed cap like a venue-run booking system would have.

## Joining a Session

### How do I join?
1. Join the Keep Calm Badminton WhatsApp group
2. Vote in the weekly poll when it goes out, so Mel knows to count you in
3. Once courts are confirmed, pay Mel by Bizum (or Revolut) to secure your spot
4. Bring your racket, indoor court shoes, water, and turn up for the confirmed time

### What if I want to come but the poll's already gone out?
Just message the group - Mel books based on numbers, so let her know as early as you can in case there's still room to add another court.

### What if I can't make it after paying?
Let Mel know **as soon as possible** so she can offer your spot to someone else - don't leave it until the last minute.

## On the Day

### What should I bring?
- **Your own racket** (ask in the group if you need to borrow one)
- **Indoor court shoes** with non-marking soles
- **Water** to stay hydrated
- Payment by **Bizum** (or Revolut) if you haven't already paid Mel for your spot

### Do I need my own racket?
Bring your own if you have one. If you need to borrow one, ask in the group beforehand - members sometimes have a spare. La Mar Bella's own reception also rents rackets for around €2 each, but stock isn't guaranteed - see [Other Venues & Gear](../other-venues-and-gear.md).

### What time should I arrive?
Aim to arrive a few minutes before the confirmed start time (shared in the group that week) so you're warmed up and ready when court time starts.

### Where exactly are the courts?
Court numbers vary week to week depending on what Mel books - check the group for that week's exact courts and directions. See [Venue](friday-badminton-venues.md).

## Safety and Etiquette

### Is it safe? Who's responsible?
Participation is **voluntary and at your own risk**. The host facilitates the session but is not responsible for participants' wellbeing or for any lost or damaged belongings. Warm up before playing, stretch afterwards, and keep your valuables with you.

### What's expected of me on court?
Rotate so everyone gets a game, keep the banter friendly and inclusive, welcome newcomers, and help set up and pack down the nets and equipment. Respect the venue staff and other users of the sports hall.

---

## Weather

### What happens if it rains?
Nothing - it's **indoor**, so the session runs rain or shine.
