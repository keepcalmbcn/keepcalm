# Friday Badminton Organisation

**Corrected 2026-07-29, refined 2026-08-02:** this session is organised
**directly by Mel**, the same manual process as the
[weekend session](../weekend-session/weekend-badminton-organisation.md).
Earlier versions of this doc described an ongoing partnership with "BCN
Badminton" via Meetup as the current process, which was inaccurate - but the
partnership itself was real and historical: **June 2026 sessions genuinely
ran through BCN Badminton's Meetup**. Since then it's been organised
directly, with no external partner community and no Meetup RSVP requirement.
Since **21 July 2026**, Mel has also been listing sessions as events on
**Keep Calm's own Meetup page** (meetup.com/keep-calm-barcelona) for
visibility, but this is a cross-post, not a booking system - payment still
runs entirely through Bizum and Mel's manual confirmed list, the same as
before.

---

## How It's Organised

- **Mel** posts a **poll in the WhatsApp group** asking who's free for the
  coming Friday.
- Based on the headcount, she books **1-3 courts** at the venue - the number
  of courts (and so the cost per person) varies week to week depending on
  interest.
- Once courts are booked, she posts the **cost per person** (calculated by
  dividing the court cost across confirmed players - typically **€5-6 per
  person**, more if multiple courts are needed) and a **Bizum** number to
  secure a spot (Revolut on request for anyone without Bizum).
- Spots are only secured once **paid**, not just by voting in the poll.
- Mel keeps a running **confirmed list** in the group (paid players marked
  with a checkmark) and updates it as people pay or drop out.

## Weekly Timeline

### Early in the week - Poll
- Post the Friday availability poll in the group.
- Ask people to update their availability as they see it, since courts are
  booked according to numbers.

### Mid-week - Confirm and book
- Once there's a good headcount, book courts (usually 1-3, depending on
  interest) at the venue.
- Share the venue, time, cost per person, and payment details (Bizum/Revolut)
  in the group.
- Keep the confirmed list updated as people pay.

### Day of - Session
- Players arrive at the venue for the confirmed time slot with their racket,
  indoor shoes, and water.
- Mel (or another organiser present) sorts out courts and rotations on the day.
- Note: the exact hall used at a venue can vary session to session (e.g. main
  hall vs a smaller side hall) - check the group on the day for exact
  directions.

## Notes for the Keep Calm Organiser

- Cost isn't fixed - it depends on how many courts get booked and how many
  people are confirmed, so it's calculated and shared fresh each week.
- A spot is only secured once the person has **paid**, so keep the confirmed
  list current and be clear that voting in the poll alone doesn't hold a
  place.
- If Mar Bella reopens and the session moves back there, update the "Current
  Temporary Note" above (or remove it) so the docs stay accurate.
