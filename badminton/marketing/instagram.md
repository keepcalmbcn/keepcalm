🏸 Social badminton for English speakers in Barcelona - mixed, friendly, all welcome.

Keep Calm Badminton is part of our not-for-profit sports community. We run two weekly sessions, Friday evening and a weekend morning, at La Mar Bella in Poblenou, right by the beach. Doubles practice games, players rotating, currently a lower-intermediate level. No pressure, just good games and good people.

Indoor courts so we play rain or shine. Typically €5-6 per person depending on the week - join the WhatsApp group and vote in the weekly poll. Bring your own racket and indoor court shoes.

Adults (18+). Find the link in bio, or join directly at keepcalm.fit/whatsapp/badminton.
