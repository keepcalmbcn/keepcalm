# Badminton - Other Venues & Gear

Practical, community-sourced answers to the questions that come up most: where else
can you play, where do you buy or rent a racket, and other logistics beyond the
[Friday session at La Mar Bella](friday-session/friday-badminton-venues.md).

Sourced from real questions and answers in the Keep Calm Badminton group, kept
generic (no names, no personal contact details) so it's safe to reuse for FAQs,
bots, or onboarding messages.

---

## Where is Keep Calm badminton played?

We run two regular sessions, **Friday evening and a weekend morning**, both
normally at **La Mar Bella Municipal Sports Complex** in Poblenou, organised
directly by Mel - see [Friday](friday-session/friday-badminton-venues.md) and
[Weekend](weekend-session/weekend-badminton-organisation.md) for full details.

## Where else can you play badminton in Barcelona?

- **BCN Badminton** - a separate Barcelona badminton community (not affiliated
  with Keep Calm) with its own [Meetup group](https://www.meetup.com/barcelona-badminton/),
  worth checking for extra games. Keep Calm's own Friday session ran through this
  Meetup back in June 2026, but that's no longer the case - see the
  [Friday organisation doc](friday-session/friday-badminton-organisation.md).
- **UPC Sports (Zona Universitària)** - the university's public sports facilities
  have **3 badminton courts**, with total court rental around **€60/hour**
  (split between whoever's playing). Official booking/info page:
  [upc.edu/esports](https://www.upc.edu/esports/ca/instal-lacions/lloguer-instal-lacions-1/esports-individuals).
  They also run drop-in badminton classes **Monday and Wednesday, 18:30-19:30**,
  around **€9.20** per drop-in, and have rackets and shuttles available on site -
  treat exact price/times as approximate and confirm directly, as these can change.
- **Estació del Nord** - has a badminton court, but booking requires **phoning
  in**, and they call you back rather than confirming instantly. Worth knowing:
  usually only the **concrete-floor court** is free, so check what surface
  you're getting before committing.
- **CEM Mar Bella** (the same complex as Keep Calm's usual Friday/weekend venue)
  also takes independent online bookings outside Keep Calm's organised sessions.
  New slots open online **at midnight**, and in summer daytime slots tend to
  vanish quickly because of kids' sports camps using the facility - if booking
  independently, it's worth being ready right at midnight.
- **Other municipal sports centres (CEMs)** around the city sometimes have
  badminton courts available to rent too, but availability, price, and how to
  book vary by venue - generally worth contacting the venue directly rather
  than assuming online booking is available.

## Where can you buy a badminton racket?

- **Decathlon** - the most commonly used option, and works best ordered online
  via [decathlon.es](https://www.decathlon.es) with next-day click-and-collect
  at your nearest store - in-store stock tends to be pretty basic, so the
  online range (including more advanced rackets, e.g. Yonex) is the better bet.
- **El Corte Inglés (Pl. Catalunya)** - doesn't stock badminton rackets at all,
  so don't count on it as an option.
- **Specialist online badminton shops** - e.g. [badminton-shop.com](https://badminton-shop.com),
  used by at least one member for a first racket.
- **Secondhand marketplaces** like Wallapop occasionally have rackets listed too,
  if you're after something cheaper to start with.

## Can you rent or borrow a racket instead of buying one?

- Members often bring a spare racket to lend at sessions - it's worth asking in
  the WhatsApp group beforehand, but don't rely on one being available.
- **La Mar Bella's own reception rents rackets** for **2€ each** - confirmed
  available as a handy backup if you turn up without one, though it's still
  worth arranging a spare in the group if you can.
