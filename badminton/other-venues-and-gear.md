# Badminton - Other Venues & Gear

Practical, community-sourced answers to the questions that come up most: where else
can you play, where do you buy or rent a racket, and other logistics beyond the
[Friday session at La Mar Bella](friday-session/friday-badminton-venues.md).

Sourced from real questions and answers in the Keep Calm Badminton group, kept
generic (no names, no personal contact details) so it's safe to reuse for FAQs,
bots, or onboarding messages.

---

## Where is Keep Calm badminton played?

We run two regular sessions, **Friday evening and a weekend morning**, both
normally at **La Mar Bella Municipal Sports Complex** in Poblenou, organised
directly by Mel - see [Friday](friday-session/friday-badminton-venues.md) and
[Weekend](weekend-session/weekend-badminton-organisation.md) for full details.

## Where else can you play badminton in Barcelona?

- **BCN Badminton** - a separate Barcelona badminton community (not affiliated
  with Keep Calm) with its own [Meetup group](https://www.meetup.com/barcelona-badminton/),
  worth checking for extra games.
- **UPC Sports (Zona Universitària)** - the university's public sports facilities
  rent out badminton courts and run drop-in classes. Official booking/info page:
  [upc.edu/esports](https://www.upc.edu/esports/ca/instal-lacions/lloguer-instal-lacions-1/esports-individuals).
  Members have reported roughly 3 courts, drop-in classes on Monday and Wednesday
  evenings, and a per-session cost without membership - treat exact price/times as
  approximate and confirm directly, as these can change.
- **Other municipal sports centres (CEMs)** around the city sometimes have
  badminton courts available to rent (e.g. Estació del Nord was mentioned), but
  availability, price, and how to book vary by venue - generally worth contacting
  the venue directly rather than assuming online booking is available.

## Where can you buy a badminton racket?

- **Decathlon** - the most commonly used option. Some physical stores (e.g. near
  L'Illa Diagonal) carry rackets in-stock, though selection there tends to be
  entry-level. For a better selection, including more advanced rackets (e.g.
  Yonex), members generally order online via [decathlon.es](https://www.decathlon.es)
  and use next-day click-and-collect at their nearest store.
- **Specialist online badminton shops** - e.g. [badminton-shop.com](https://badminton-shop.com),
  used by at least one member for a first racket.
- **Secondhand marketplaces** like Wallapop occasionally have rackets listed too,
  if you're after something cheaper to start with.

## Can you rent or borrow a racket instead of buying one?

- Members often bring a spare racket to lend at sessions - it's worth asking in
  the WhatsApp group beforehand, but don't rely on one being available.
- **La Mar Bella's own reception rents rackets** for around **2€ each** - a handy
  backup if you turn up without one, though stock isn't guaranteed, so still worth
  arranging a spare in the group if you can.
