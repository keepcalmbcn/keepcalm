# 🌅 Sunset Paddleboarding - Recap (18 July 2026)

Our first ever Keep Calm Watersports event was a sell-out: all 20 spots went
within about 24 hours of the sign-up sheet opening, with a waiting list behind
them. Huge thanks to Mitali for arranging the group rate and running the event,
and to Paddle Surf Brasil BCN for hosting us out on the water.

## How it went

- A group met at the beach near the venue from around 17:00 for a pre-event
  hangout, then everyone gathered at Paddle Surf Brasil BCN at 19:00
- Life jackets, boards, and paddles were all provided by the venue - people
  just brought swimwear and towels
- Out on the boards from 19:30 through sunset, guided by the venue's
  instructors, with drinks afterwards for those who stayed
- Photos from the water were shared in the group afterwards, and the event
  made it onto [our Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/p/Da8wx00ClL6/)

## Lessons for next time

- **The in-chat numbered sign-up sheet worked but was messy** - several people
  accidentally deleted each other's names while copy-pasting the list, and it
  needed admin cleanup. Future events should use a Google Form (which also
  captures the waiver and data notice) - see the note in [readme.md](../readme.md)
- **Demand comfortably exceeded supply** - the venue ran out of boards, and
  latecomers had to be turned away. Worth asking the venue about capacity for
  a bigger group, or running events more often
- **Spot transfers worked well** - a cancellation the day before was reposted
  to the group and the spot was picked up within the hour
- Paddle Surf Brasil BCN gives Keep Calm a preferred group rate and is happy
  to host us again - they are the default venue for future SUP events
