# Keep Calm Women's Football - Group Description

The following is used as the WhatsApp group description.

---

Keep Calm Women's Football - part of the Keep Calm community for English speakers in Barcelona.

⚽👯‍♀️ A brand new women-only football (soccer) group for players of all levels. A relaxed, supportive space to play together, whether you have never kicked a ball or you have played your whole life.

Why a women's group? Lots of women told us they wanted somewhere to start out and play together first, so we made it. It runs alongside our mixed Friday football, not instead of it - you are very welcome at both.

This group is brand new, so there is no fixed schedule yet. Watch this space for the first session announcement, and have a say in the day, time, and format as we get it up and running.

Open to adults only (18+).
