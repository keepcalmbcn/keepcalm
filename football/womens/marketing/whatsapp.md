⚽👯‍♀️ *New: Keep Calm Women's Football!*

Following on from Women's Padel and Women's Volleyball, we've just opened a brand new women-only football (soccer) group in Barcelona.

*What to expect:*
- All levels welcome, whether you've never kicked a ball or played your whole life
- A relaxed, no-pressure space to get a game together with other women
- Runs alongside our mixed Friday football, not instead of it
- Format and schedule still being decided, so early joiners get a real say

It's brand new, so right now it's about getting enough of us together to pick a day, time and format that works. If you've been wanting to play, come help shape it from the start.

Part of Keep Calm, a not-for-profit English-speaking community in Barcelona.

👉 Join at keepcalm.fit/whatsapp/womans-football

18+ only.
